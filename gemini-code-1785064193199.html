About The Corner

A Quiet Introduction, Not a Retail Shelf.

01What Is This?

The Caffeina Experience Corner is a curated coffee display placed inside selected partner locations. It is not a retail shelf; it is a quiet introduction to The Caffeina's world of coffee, culture, and connection.

02Why You Are Seeing It Here

You are seeing this corner at Auto Worx, our Experience Partner #001. While your car is being cared for, this corner invites you to take a quiet moment for yourself.

03Experience Partner #001

Auto Worx — Bashundhara Gate. Our first offline Experience Corner, created as a pilot for selected premium waiting spaces.

  • Easy pour-over brewing
  • Designed as a premium coffee experience

How It Works

No Machine Needed.

01
Open the drip sachet
Tear open carefully to remove the drip coffee bag.
02
Place it on your cup
Secure the drip coffee bag on your cup.
03
Pour hot water slowly
Pour in small circles, 90–96°C. Brew 2–3 times until the bag is empty.

No machine needed. Just hot water, a cup, and a few quiet minutes.
